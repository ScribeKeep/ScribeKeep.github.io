ScribeKeep Support

Your words. Your notes. Your cloud.

Thank you for using ScribeKeep.

ScribeKeep is designed to record meetings, lectures, interviews, discussions, and other spoken notes, create transcripts, and let you keep your content locally or export it to your own supported cloud-storage account.

Recording and Transcription

ScribeKeep records audio locally on your iPhone and creates a transcript using supported Apple speech technologies.

Transcription accuracy can vary depending on:

  • Speaker clarity
  • Distance from the iPhone
  • Background noise
  • Room echo
  • Multiple people speaking at the same time
  • Specialized terminology

If a transcript contains an error, you can refer to the original recording and edit the transcript.

Google Drive

Google Drive is optional.

To connect:

  1. Open ScribeKeep Settings.
  2. Select Google Drive.
  3. Choose Connect.
  4. Sign in with your Google account.
  5. Approve the requested permissions.

When you save a note to Google Drive, ScribeKeep can create a Google Doc containing your transcript and optionally upload the original audio recording.

Your Google account owns and controls the exported files.

Disconnecting Google Drive

You can disconnect Google Drive from within ScribeKeep.

You can also revoke ScribeKeep's access through your Google account security settings.

Disconnecting ScribeKeep does not delete files that you already saved to Google Drive.

Managing Local Storage

ScribeKeep shows the size of retained audio recordings so you can identify files that are using significant storage.

You may remove a local audio recording while keeping:

  • The note
  • Transcript
  • Written notes
  • Date and duration
  • Cloud-export information

If an audio recording has not been backed up elsewhere, removing it permanently deletes that local audio file.

Privacy

ScribeKeep is designed around user-owned storage.

Recordings and transcripts remain local unless you explicitly choose to export them to a supported cloud provider.

Read the full ScribeKeep Privacy Policy.
