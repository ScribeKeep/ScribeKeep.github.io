ScribeKeep Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 3, 2026

ScribeKeep is designed to give you control over your recordings, transcripts, and notes. This Privacy Policy explains how ScribeKeep handles information when you use the app.

Information ScribeKeep Processes

ScribeKeep may process the following information on your device:

  • Audio recordings you create
  • Transcripts generated from those recordings
  • Speaker labels and names you assign
  • Notes and titles you enter
  • Recording dates, times, durations, and file sizes
  • App preferences and settings

ScribeKeep does not require you to create a ScribeKeep account.

Audio Recordings and Transcripts

Audio recordings are stored locally on your device unless you choose to export them.

Transcription is designed to occur on your device using Apple-provided speech-recognition technologies where supported.

ScribeKeep does not operate a proprietary cloud service for storing your recordings or transcripts.

Cloud Storage

ScribeKeep allows you to export content to cloud-storage services you choose.

The current version supports Google Drive and Google Docs.

When you choose to connect Google Drive, Google authentication is handled through Google's authorization services. ScribeKeep receives only the permissions necessary to perform the cloud-storage functions you authorize.

When you choose to export a note, ScribeKeep may send the following to your Google account:

  • Transcript text
  • Note title and associated metadata
  • Optional written notes
  • Audio recording, if you choose to include it

ScribeKeep does not upload your recordings or transcripts to Google unless you explicitly initiate a cloud export.

Your use of Google services is also subject to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

Google Account Information

When you connect Google Drive, ScribeKeep may receive basic account information necessary to display your connection status, such as your Google account email address.

ScribeKeep does not receive or store your Google account password.

Authentication tokens are handled using supported Google and Apple authentication mechanisms.

Data Collection by the Developer

ScribeKeep does not currently use advertising SDKs or analytics services to collect your recording or transcript content.

The developer does not receive copies of your recordings, transcripts, or notes through normal use of the app.

Local Storage and Deletion

Your recordings and notes remain on your device until you delete them or choose settings that remove local audio after a successful cloud export.

You may:

  • Delete an entire note
  • Remove a local audio recording while retaining its transcript
  • Disconnect your Google account
  • Revoke ScribeKeep's Google access through your Google account settings

Deleting local content from ScribeKeep does not automatically delete copies you previously exported to your own cloud-storage account.

Transcription Accuracy

Automated transcription may not always be exact. Accuracy can vary based on speaker clarity, background noise, distance, overlapping speech, accents, and terminology.

When accuracy is important, retain the original audio recording so you can verify or correct the transcript.

Microphone and Speech Recognition Permissions

ScribeKeep requests microphone access in order to record audio.

The app may also request speech-recognition permissions needed to generate transcripts using Apple-provided technologies.

You may manage these permissions through iOS Settings.

Children's Privacy

ScribeKeep is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because ScribeKeep does not operate a user-account or proprietary cloud-storage service, the developer does not normally receive user recording or transcript content.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as ScribeKeep adds features or cloud-storage options.

Any material changes will be reflected on this page and the effective date will be updated.
