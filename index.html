What ScribeKeep does

Record & Transcribe

Record meetings, lectures, interviews, discussions, and other spoken notes while ScribeKeep creates a transcript.

Keep the Source

Retain the original audio so you can verify or correct important details when needed.

Use Your Own Cloud

Keep content locally or export supported files to your own cloud account rather than a required proprietary ScribeKeep cloud.

Ways to use ScribeKeep

Study smarter

Record lectures, save transcripts to Google Drive, then use Gemini or another tool of your choice to turn them into summaries, study guides, flashcards, practice questions, or review material.

Prepare for meetings

Keep searchable transcripts of meetings for follow-up, planning, decisions, and future reference.

Capture interviews and discussions

Separate speakers, rename them afterward, and preserve both the transcript and original recording.

Build your own workflow

ScribeKeep does not lock your notes into a proprietary cloud. Export your content and continue working with the tools you already prefer.
